# Proposed membership terms — attorney review draft (NOT FOR CUSTOMER ACCEPTANCE)
Orange Aesthetics & Wellness is a separate business from Pacific Urgent Care & Family Medicine. No Pacific services, insurance-billed services or independent partner services are discounted.

## Plans
- Wellness: $99/month; one Mini PlenishIV service entitlement per paid billing cycle; 15% off eligible Orange Aesthetics services and retail.
- Performance: $199/month; one qualifying drip per paid billing cycle; 20% off eligible Orange Aesthetics services and retail.
- Elite: $399/month; two qualifying drips per paid billing cycle; 25% off eligible Orange Aesthetics services and retail; priority scheduling subject to availability.
- NAD+ separately purchased; eligible plan discount may apply; no public medication price guarantees.
- Discounts do not stack with promotions unless expressly authorized; products may be excluded if manufacturer/contract/law restricts discounting. Publish a complete exclusion list before enrollment.

## Included IV benefit lifecycle — proposed, not yet enforceable
1. Upon successful payment verification for a new monthly billing period, grant the appropriate number of nontransferable *service-use entitlements*; not a dollar wallet, gift certificate, or separately sold treatment package.
2. Proposed eligibility: grant issuance through 90 calendar days after issuance, timestamped in America/Los_Angeles for display and stored as UTC instants. No expiration unless counsel approves classification and exact wording.
3. Use earliest-expiring eligible entitlement first; do not consume expired entitlements; prohibit negative balances and double use.
4. Expired grants remain in owner-only protected historical reports and are never silently deleted. Reinstatement/refund creates an approved, traceable adjustment with original records retained.
5. Member portal shows available credits, upcoming dates, redemption history and expired history. Do not claim credits were redeemed if they merely expired.
6. Eligibility depends on medical screening and appropriate clinician authorization; a purchase or benefit never guarantees treatment.
7. Define cancellation, pauses, failed payment, late renewal, medical ineligibility, clinic cancellation, unavailable services, refunds, chargebacks, and accessibility accommodations before signing. Where legal status is uncertain, preserve benefit/value and escalate rather than forfeiting.
8. Purchased gift cards and separately prepaid treatment packages must have their own legally reviewed terms and ledger, not this expiration rule.

## Recurring billing and consent — required UX
Show plan price, billing cadence, benefits, discount limitations, renewal and cancellation terms before payment; obtain distinct affirmative consent; issue a retainable confirmation; preserve consent version and timestamp; support online cancellation; send legally required reminders and notices. Do not launch until attorney-approved.
