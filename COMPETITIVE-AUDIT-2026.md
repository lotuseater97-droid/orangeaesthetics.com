# Orange Aesthetics & Wellness — Orange County Competitive Website Audit
Research date: 2026-11-21. Representative public-site sample, **not** an exhaustive census of every med spa in Orange County.

## Competitor websites and observed strengths
- NuVie MD (Irvine): https://www.nuviemd.com/ — extensive service taxonomy, explore by treatment/concern, prominent consultation booking, editorial visual identity.
- Concierge Aesthetics (Irvine): https://www.conciergeaesthetics.com/ — branded clinic photography, review proof, financing disclosure, consultation CTA, welcome-list offer.
- Alladerm (Aliso Viejo): https://alladerm.com/ — first-visit incentive and $99/$149 aesthetic memberships.
- Clinical Aesthetics (Villa Park): https://www.clinicalaestheticsskincare.com/ — seasonal specials, monthly facial membership, prices.
- OnSite Wellness Now (Orange): https://onsitewellnessnow.com/ — NP-led positioning, transparent appointment flow, financing and bilingual information.
- XO Medical Spa (Tustin): https://www.xomedspa.com/services — priced bundles and annual VIP membership.
- Masters Medspa (Irvine): https://masters-medspa.com/ — aesthetics + wellness brand positioning and online booking.
- Mirako Spa (Garden Grove/Brea): https://mirakospa.com/ — visible promotions, gift-card campaigns, broad service discovery.
- Skin Heaven (Anaheim): https://www.gotoskinheaven.com/ — single-session laser offers and financing links.
- OC Cosmetic and Vein Center: https://occosmeticandvein.com/storage/2025/12/Final-Price-Menu-Dec25.pdf — published comparison price menu; Botox $12/unit, microneedling $300/session and 3 for $750, PRP microneedling $450/session; laser six-session packages from $150 small area.
- BioSpa (Newport Beach): https://www.mybiospa.com/ — established reputation and broad treatment coverage.
- DermFx (Orange): https://www.dermfx.com/treatments/ — breadth of services and strong local presence.

## Completed in website
- Coastal editorial design, responsive layout, photo-backed hero and service tiles.
- Balanced aesthetics and IV wellness homepage; laser is a treatment category, not the homepage identity.
- Botox $11/unit (owner approved); Pacific IV menu retained.
- Treatment-by-concern discovery, first-visit explanation, FAQs, mobile call CTA.
- Local business schema, sitemap and robots.txt.
- Separate laser page explaining a single-session introductory concept, without publishing an unapproved Groupon price.

## Important missing items / next release
1. **Verify deployed Cloudflare website and photography**: current images use third-party Unsplash URLs and have not been checked for correct loading, licensing, image-subject suitability or performance. Replace with licensed self-hosted assets; use an approved real provider portrait only on the provider page. Do not misrepresent stock images as the clinic.
2. **Booking**: connect actual scheduling system/approved booking URL; no placeholder booking links or unsecured PHI intake.
3. **Authentic trust**: real clinic photos, provider bio and credentials verified, verified Google review link and permissioned testimonials/before-after gallery. Never invent testimonials or outcomes.
4. **Pricing**: approve aesthetic menu, area definitions and package terms; preserve Botox $11/unit and published Pacific IV prices. Groupon single-session rate and commission to be negotiated.
5. **Revenue programs**: decide whether to offer an aesthetic maintenance membership, gift cards, prepaid packages and financing; confirm profitability and legal compliance before advertising.
6. **SEO & usability**: validate all links, image accessibility, mobile layout, page performance, structured data and search indexing; submit sitemap to Search Console.
7. **Compliance**: verify professional practice disclosures, clinical supervision/ownership structure, California medical advertising rules, consent, privacy policy, cancellation policy, and treatment-specific risk statements.
8. **Conversion tracking**: privacy-aware click-to-call, bookings, referral source, Groupon customer retention and treatment conversion; no patient-identifiable data in generic analytics.
9. **Seasonal promotions**: optional rotating offers that do not undercut margin or imply unapproved services.
10. **Bilingual pages**: consider Spanish after accurate professional review; do not claim staff speaks Spanish without confirmation.

## Positioning
Premium accessible med spa; personalized clinician-led aesthetics + IV wellness. Acquire clients with a **single laser session Groupon**, retain them through quality care and optional multi-session treatments, not pressure tactics. Avoid claims about specific devices or treatments until equipment and scope are verified.

## Research caution
Competitor prices and promotions change frequently. Use observed public prices for planning only, not as Orange Aesthetics approved offers.
