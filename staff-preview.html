DESIGN PREVIEW — NO REAL PATIENT ACCESS. Camera scanning is limited to a clearly marked demo token. No login, patient lookup, entitlement check, credit redemption or clinical clearance is connected. Do not scan real member credentials or enter patient details.

1. Scan a member card

In production, staff will scan a short-lived, opaque QR token. A secure server will resolve the member and require two-identifier verification.

Enter OA-DEMO-CARD to preview the flow. No real member IDs are accepted.

No demo card checked.

2. Verify member identity

Before a real check-in, staff must verify two identifiers in a protected system. A QR code alone never proves identity.

3. Check membership and credits

  • Confirm subscription payment status.
  • Check current and rollover credits, expiry and eligible services.
  • Confirm no duplicate redemption.
  • Separate billing eligibility from clinician clearance.

4. Complete visit

Production workflow will record a check-in, obtain clinical approval separately, and redeem an eligible credit through an atomic, audited server transaction.

Staff permissions

Staff can check in and verify billing eligibility. They cannot view owner financial reports, grant credits, bypass clinician approval or issue unauthorized refunds.
