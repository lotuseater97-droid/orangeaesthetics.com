Two approaches to skin rejuvenation.

Traditional microneedling

Controlled micro-injuries stimulate the skin's repair response. Discuss texture, scarring and individualized expectations.

RF microneedling

Combines needles with radiofrequency energy. Treatment suitability depends on skin, goals and clinical assessment.

Recovery & risks

Redness and downtime can occur. Potential complications include infection, scarring, burns and pigment changes.

The clinic historically used a MINAS-family RF device; the exact model and current service availability require confirmation. Ask for current pricing before scheduling.
