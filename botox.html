Ask about Botox

The treatment

A refreshed look, still you.

Botox is a prescription neuromodulator that temporarily reduces activity in selected facial muscles. Common cosmetic treatment areas include forehead lines, lines between the brows and crow's feet. Dosing is individualized, so the total price depends on the units used.

What to expect

A consultation, facial assessment, discussion of your goals and treatment when appropriate.

Timing

Effects develop over several days and are temporary; duration varies.

Safety first

Bruising, asymmetry and eyelid droop can occur. Rare serious adverse effects are possible.

Botox pricing is $11 per unit. Dysport and Xeomin dosing and prices are separate and must be confirmed; units are not interchangeable.
