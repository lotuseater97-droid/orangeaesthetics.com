What the evidence means

Requires review means the exact product, concentration, carrier fluid, and administration method have not been verified together. It does not mean the ingredients are known to react. Blocked is reserved for an established restriction or a clinic ordering rule, such as putting a separately administered glutathione push into an infusion bag.

Historical clinic mixing guides are useful comparisons, but they are not proof of stability. The California Board of Pharmacy also warns that elective sterile admixture preparation can fall under sterile-compounding requirements. Every request is reviewed by a clinician before treatment.
